How the model thinks

Two classic machine learning techniques, combined. Each one is simple — together they're surprisingly powerful.

Step 01
📝
Your message comes in
Raw SMS text, exactly as typed. No preprocessing needed — the model handles it.
Step 02
🔢
TF-IDF converts words to numbers
Words like "FREE" and "WINNER" score high in spam. "Dentist" and "lunch" score high in real messages.
Step 03
🧮
Naive Bayes calculates probability
Multiplies the spam-score of every word together to produce a final percentage. Fast and accurate.
Step 04
✅
You get a verdict + confidence
Spam or Ham — with a confidence score so you know how certain the model is.
Batch checker — test multiple messages at once
